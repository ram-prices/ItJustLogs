Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

ItJustLogs (itjustlogs.dev) is a free, fan-made pull-history tracker for Arknights: Endfield. It has no accounts and no server of its own: it runs entirely in your browser. This page explains what it stores, where, and what it shares.

What stays in your browser

  • Your pull history (the file you import or paste), your settings, and any profile picture you add.
  • These are kept in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on your device. They are never sent to ItJustLogs or anyone else, except to your own Google Drive if you turn on sync (below).
  • You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete saved data, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose Sign in with Google in Settings, ItJustLogs uses Google's sign-in to sync your data between your devices:

  • It asks for one permission, drive.appdata: access to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that belongs to ItJustLogs alone. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • It stores one file there, a backup of your pulls, settings and profile picture, and reads it back on your other devices.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is syncing. That email is kept in your browser while sync is on; the short-lived access token from Google is kept only for the current browser tab.
  • All of this happens between your browser and Google. The developer of ItJustLogs never receives your Google data, your token or your backup.
  • To stop: choose Sign out in Settings. To remove the Drive copy too, delete ItJustLogs' hidden app data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps), and you can remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ItJustLogs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide sync to you, and is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone.

The pull-history exporter

The optional PowerShell script shown in Settings reads the game's own cache on your PC to find your history link, and downloads your history only from Gryphline's official server (ef-webview.gryphline.com). It saves the result to your Desktop and clipboard. It sends nothing anywhere else.

Other services the site loads from

To show the page, your browser loads files from these services, which receive your IP address as any website does:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the typeface and icons.
  • GitHub and jsDelivr (raw.githubusercontent.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net) for banner names, the schedule and game art.
  • Gryphline's image server (web-static.hg-cdn.com) for art of new banners.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site.

ItJustLogs itself uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Children

ItJustLogs doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age: your data stays with you.
